About Tidewater

Tidewater started in Bristol in 2021. Our founders spent ten years as bookkeepers for small businesses and saw the same problem every month: good work, sent on time, paid late because the invoice was confusing or the reminder never went out.

We built Tidewater to fix the dull part of getting paid. A clear invoice, an easy way to pay, and reminders that go out without anyone having to remember them.

What we believe

Small businesses should not need an accountant to send an invoice. Prices should be simple and shown up front. Your customers' details belong to you, and you can export everything at any time.

The team

We are a team of twelve in Bristol and Cardiff, and we answer support emails ourselves. If you want to talk to us, the contact page has every way to reach us.
