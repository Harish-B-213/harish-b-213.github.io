Features

Tidewater covers the whole job from the first invoice to the money in your bank. These are the parts our customers use every day.

Invoice templates

Start from one of six clean templates and add your logo and colours. Every invoice gets a number in sequence, a due date from your payment terms, and the VAT worked out line by line.

Online payments

Connect Stripe or GoCardless once. Every invoice then carries a pay button, and paid invoices close themselves. Card fees are shown before you switch it on, so there are no surprises.

Automatic reminders

Set up to five reminders, before and after the due date. Each one is sent from your own address with your own wording. You can read our late payment reminder templates to get started.

Recurring invoices

Retainers, subscriptions and monthly maintenance contracts go out on the day you choose, with the next invoice created automatically.

Reports and exports

See what is paid, due and overdue at a glance. Export to Xero, QuickBooks or CSV, with quarterly VAT totals ready for your return.
