Questions people ask

Is Tidewater free?

Your first 10 invoices are free, with every feature switched on. After that, plans start at £9 a month.

Can my customers pay without an account?

Yes. They open the invoice from the email and pay with a card or bank transfer. They never need to sign up.

Does it work with my accounting software?

Tidewater connects to Xero and QuickBooks, and exports CSV for anything else.
