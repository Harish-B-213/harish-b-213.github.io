How to write an invoice

An invoice is a request for payment. A good one is clear, complete and easy to pay.

What to include on an invoice

  1. Your business name and contact details.
  2. The client's name and address.
  3. A unique invoice number.
  4. Invoice date and due date.
  5. An itemised list of work with prices.
  6. Total due, taxes and accepted payment methods.

Choosing payment terms

Net 14 and Net 30 are the most common. Shorter terms get you paid sooner; state late fees clearly if you charge them.
