Late payment reminder email templates

Most late payments are forgotten, not refused. A friendly reminder usually works.

1. A day after the due date

Hi {name}, a quick reminder that invoice #{number} was due yesterday. You can pay here: {link}.

2. Seven days overdue

Hi {name}, invoice #{number} is now a week overdue. Could you let me know when to expect payment?

3. Fourteen days overdue

Hi {name}, I have not yet received payment for invoice #{number}. Please settle it by Friday.

4. Thirty days overdue

Hi {name}, invoice #{number} is 30 days overdue. Late fees apply from next week as per our terms.

5. Final notice

Hi {name}, this is a final notice for invoice #{number} before we pass it to collections.
