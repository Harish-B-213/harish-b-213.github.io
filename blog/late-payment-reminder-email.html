Late payment reminder email: 5 templates

Published 9 May 2022

Most late invoices are not refused, they are forgotten. A clear, friendly reminder at the right time gets most of them paid within a few days. These five templates go from a gentle nudge before the due date to a final notice.

1. Before the due date

"Hi Sam, a quick reminder that invoice 1042 for £480 is due on Friday. You can pay by card or bank transfer from the link in the invoice. Thanks!"

2. On the due date

"Hi Sam, invoice 1042 for £480 is due today. If it is already on its way, thank you, and please ignore this email."

3. One week late

"Hi Sam, invoice 1042 for £480 was due last Friday. Could you let me know when I can expect payment?"

4. Two weeks late

"Hi Sam, invoice 1042 is now two weeks overdue. Please pay by Friday, or reply if there is a problem with the invoice."

5. Final notice

"Hi Sam, invoice 1042 is 30 days overdue. Under the Late Payment of Commercial Debts Act I may add interest and a fixed fee if it is not paid within seven days."
