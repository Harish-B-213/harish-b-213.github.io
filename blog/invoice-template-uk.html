Free invoice template for UK businesses

Published 11 January 2023

Looking for an invoice template? Every UK invoice needs an invoice number, your details, your customer's details, the date, what you sold, the total and your payment terms. VAT registered businesses also need their VAT number and the VAT on each line.

Copy these fields into a document and you have a template. For the full step-by-step guide, read how to write an invoice.
